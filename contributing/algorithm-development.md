# Algorithm Development

Detailed algorithm-development documentation is kept in private internal docs, not in this public repository.

Public docs may describe product capabilities at a high level, but they should not include:

- proprietary detection logic
- scoring or confidence-calibration details
- thresholds or tuning guidance
- benchmark or dataset internals
- patent or research strategy notes

If you are working on internal NeuralDiff analysis systems, keep that material in the daemon/api project's internal docs.
